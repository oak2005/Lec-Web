Who We Are

A trusted Nigerian name in premium paints and interior finishing.

Lavish Exclusive Chemical & Interior Concept (LEC) specializes in the production and supply of premium decorative and protective paints, industrial paint chemicals, and complete building finishing solutions.

We combine innovation, craftsmanship, and premium-quality materials to deliver exceptional results for residential, commercial, and industrial projects. Beyond paint manufacturing, LEC also provides professional interior design, renovation, property finishing, and space transformation services tailored to each client's vision.

  • Premium decorative & protective paints
  • Industrial paint chemicals
  • Interior design & styling
  • Building & property finishing
